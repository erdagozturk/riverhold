2026-09-30 — Playable prototype update

Implemented:
- 18 stack-limited augments plus same-rarity emergency fallback cards.
- Every 30 seconds, a single shared rarity roll: Silver 55%, Gold 33%, Prismatic 12%.
- Both teams receive three distinct cards from that tier. Player chooses; AI chooses randomly.
- Modifiers apply to existing and future units, independently per team.
- Added counters: tank receives 55% arrow damage; fast unit deals 135% damage to ranged; tank deals 120% damage to fast units.
- Healing, critical hits, tank splash, protective aura, melee lifesteal and one-use low-health recovery.
- Six-slot FIFO recruitment queue. Gold reserved once, production 1.0/1.8/2.6 seconds, training augment reduces duration.
- Role-specific KayKit Rogue, Ranger and Barbarian, hand-slot weapons, shared animation libraries, bow draw/release and projectile origin.
- Portrait recruitment deck, cost/affordability state, training progress, army commands, rarity choice cards, owned effects and damaged-unit health bars.
- Larger gold coins; removed floating +15 label.
- Six-minute visual day/night cycle with brighter torches at night. No gameplay lighting advantage.
- Billboard noise flame replaces solid sphere. Subtle team rim glow preserves character colors.
- Highlands Barracks plus four additional huts per village. Roads and vegetation exclusion share authored entrances. Both teams share symmetric footprints.
- Road soil/edge fading and bridge dirt overlay. Existing modular houses and castle retained.

Validation:
- 600 randomized offers: correct shared rarity, three unique choices, capped-pool fallback.
- Existing and newly spawned units receive the correct team's upgrades.
- Ranged resistance, bow attachment/animation, pause/resume and recruitment one-time charge tests passed.
- 40-unit deployment, symmetrical terrain, unobstructed roads and altar victory regression passed.
- Main combat smoke checks passed after updating the obsolete fixed +20% card assertion.
- GPU rendered HUD, card screen, day/night and close-up character models; portrait sizing and team tint corrected.

- Current flame/wear effects and village composition are an improvement pass, not a match to the reference's finished art quality.
